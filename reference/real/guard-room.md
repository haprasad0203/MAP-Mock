# Main gate and guard room: real-building sheet (16 Nov 2026)
Montage [guard-room.jpg](guard-room.jpg).
- **Gate portal over the road:**
  - a long **green hip roof** with deep eaves, on two rows of **square white columns** (about 7 m high);
  - a raised central **clerestory box** with its own small hip roof;
  - the fascia reads **"INDIAN INSTITUTE OF MANAGEMENT SHILLONG"** between two emblems.
- **Guard room:** a small white room under the left (inbound) side of the portal, with a door and a window.
- **Gate:** a brown steel **sliding gate** with a curved top, across the road.
- **Name wall:** a **sandstone** wall with the names in Hindi and English and the emblem, right of the gate.
- **Colours:** roof #2F7568, columns #E6E1D6, stone #B08A62, gate #6A5440.
- **Gaps:** the inner face; the exact span (the data has only the guard-room box and the gate landmark).
