# Hostel 1, D Block: real-building sheet (16 Nov 2026)
Montage [hostel-1.jpg](hostel-1.jpg), fit [hostel-1_fit.png](hostel-1_fit.png). The five data parts sit on the real long blocks within about 4 m.
- **Floors:** 4 (ground + 3), about 3.3 m each. The ground floor and plinth are stone-clad where the ground falls (DSC_9111).
- **Walls:** **white** plaster.
- **Long faces:**
  - projecting **balconies** in pairs, each with a **black steel rail** and a curved white slab edge;
  - **grey louvred panels** (vertical slats) between the balcony pairs.
- **End walls:** small square windows.
- **Roof:** **flat**, finished with a thin **projecting white slab** and no parapet. The deck looks dark from above (solar panels or waterproofing).
- **Stair towers:** white, rising a little above the roof, with tall narrow windows.
- **Colours:** walls #F2F1EC, rails #2B2E30, louvres #8E9497, stone #A89A88.
- **Surroundings:** the covered walkway (Scholar's Path) and its link to the Mess; courts and the football ground below.
- **Gaps:** which faces carry the balconies (assumed: both long faces), the entrance, the end walls straight on.
