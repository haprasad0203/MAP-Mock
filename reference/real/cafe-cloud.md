# Cafe Cloud Campus: real-building sheet (16 Nov 2026)
Montage [cafe-cloud.jpg](cafe-cloud.jpg), fit [cafe-cloud_fit.png](cafe-cloud_fit.png). The data outline lies within the 5 m fit error. The real octagon is about 30 m across, the same as the data.
- **Form:** a one-storey octagon (about 4.5 m) on a 0.6 m plinth. A continuous **window band** runs all round between slim white piers, under a deep **white fascia ring** (about 1.2 m) that reads as a parapet.
- **Roof:**
  - a low **green-teal octagonal roof** inside the fascia ring;
  - above it, a raised **octagonal clerestory** (white, with a window band, about 40 % of the width) under a **green octagonal pyramid**;
  - two grey roof units by the clerestory.
- **Porch:** a white rectangular **porch block** on the road (east) side, about 8 × 6 m and the same height as the fascia.
- **Colours:** walls #EEEAE2, roof #2F7A6C (DJI, in sun), glass dark teal.
- **Surroundings:** pale paver apron, steps down to the main road, trees to the west.
- **Gaps:** ground photos of any face; the door position (taken as the porch, by eye).
