## Facts by building and item

### Admin Block
Sources: 2025 film @0:06 (straight-on front), IIMS @0:42, @1:50, @7:02, @7:34, the drone nadir.

**Front, west to east:**
1. **West end block:** white, 4 floors, sandstone ground floor, a full-height glass strip near its inner edge.
2. **West wing:** 3 floors of continuous corridor balconies. Each floor has a **teal-blue canopy band**. Honey-tan split-stone piers between the bays.
3. **Centre block:** a white frame with glass bands (3 bays a floor) over a portico of tall slender white columns.
4. **East wing:** like the west wing.
5. **East end block:** white, with the emblem (about 4.5 m) and "IIM SHILLONG" in green, and a glass strip.

**Roofs:** teal-green corrugated hip roofs (about #2E8B76 in good light) with deep white fascias. The rear hall is one large square hip roof, about 43 m, behind the centre block (nadir).

**Forecourt:**
- red-pink pavers;
- a teardrop lawn with a box-hedge edge;
- a ring round the flag base and paths from the ring;
- a very tall flag mast (about 30 m) with a gold finial;
- tall solar lamps with panels on top round the drive;
- clipped conifers and flower beds;
- a beige checker-paved parking plaza on the east.

### NAB
Sources: Admin/IMG_6670, 2025 @0:02, New Videos 073650, NAB_Outside, the drone nadir.

**SW end tower:**
- honey-tan split-stone cladding;
- a full-height corner glass bay;
- a narrow vertical glass strip;
- a two-tier white pavilion on top with green hip caps.

**White blocks:** small square windows, and a taller white stair tower with vertical slits.

**Centre:** a full-height teal glass curtain wall.

**Entrance:**
- the emblem on a rose-tan stone pier between two dark glass curtain walls;
- a flat white canopy;
- a dark granite planter;
- steel railings.

**Roofs:** flat and grey, with **green pyramid caps, which are the skylights over the internal atria** (NAB interiors show lawn courts under skylights).

**Paving:** red and cream zigzag pavers in a checker pattern on the plaza.

### Walk from Admin to NAB
- An open paved path of light grey pavers (no canopy: H A).
- Flat round ring-head lamps on dark poles, in pairs on both sides about 9 m apart.
- Wooden benches with black iron legs, and young palms.

### Scholar's Path (covered walkway along the main road)
Sources: New Videos 073418, 072808, 074849.
- Brown steel posts.
- A translucent pale corrugated roof (it reads light blue-grey from the air).
- Cream X-pattern railings between the posts.
- Low stone-clad walls and benches.
- A red paver floor.

### Main gate
Source: IIMS @0:22.
- A wide portal under a large sage-green gable roof on cream square pillars.
- "INDIAN INSTITUTE OF MANAGEMENT SHILLONG" in metal letters on the beam, with an emblem medallion at each end.
- Cream gates with vertical bars and arched tops.
- Glazed guard cabins on both sides.
- A stone-clad name wall with Hindi and English letters and the emblem.
- Red pavers, and low stone walls with hedges.

### Cafe Cloud Campus (CCC)
- **The octagon:**
  - a peach-cream flat-roofed portico with round columns and the sign "CAFE CLOUD CAMPUS · EAT, CHILL, REPEAT";
  - a stone plinth and steps;
  - the octagonal hall with a radial timber ceiling.
- **Next to it (nadir):** Amul Cafe (the teal kiosk), a red-roofed hut, a blue shed, and a grey-roofed store to the north.

### Hostels (D Block area)
- **D Block:**
  - long cream blocks with peach bands and balcony rows;
  - sage-green low roofs with white fascias;
  - white stair towers;
  - steel-framed rooftop water-tank platforms with green roofs.
- **Covered walkways:** green corrugated roofs on white posts with green railings, following the hairpin road.
- **Terraces:** red-paver terraces with green steel railings.
- **Small buildings:** a single-storey cream block with a green roof, and a white container by the road.
- Construction frames on the slopes.

### Lake
Sources: 2025 @1:06 aerial, New Videos 075240 and 075321, CCC_and_Lake.
- Long, narrow and winding (river-like).
- Stone-pitched banks and a red-earth path along them.
- The road and walkway run along the east bank.
- Pines round it.
- Water: muddy brown in June, blue-green in the dry months.

### Season (H A: late November, cherry blossom)
- Dense pale-pink Himalayan cherry (IIMS @0:10) among the pines.
- Grass is green going golden in the dry months.
- The lake is clear blue-green.
