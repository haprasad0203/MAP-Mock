## Footprint and fit
- **Data parts:**
  - part 0 + part 1 = **west end block** (27 × 13 m and 23 × 22 m);
  - part 2 = **centre range** (the centre block, both linking wings and the hall behind, 60 × 44 m, drawn by eye);
  - part 3 = **east end block** (24 × 14 m).
- **Fit** (`admin_fit.png`, hi-res satellite 0423 with a pin fit of 1.99 px/m, residual about 5 m): all four parts sit on the real roofs within the fit error. The south edge of part 2 runs along the real wing fronts. Part 2 also covers the large dark roof behind the centre block, which is the **hall Google labels "IIM Shillong Auditorium"**. That pin sits on the Admin Block itself, not on a separate building. **Proposal for LAYOUT_CHECK:** the Auditorium is the rear hall of the Admin Block.
- No footprint changes are proposed. The part 2 outline is by eye but agrees with the satellite.

- **Floor height** about **3.7 m** (window-row spacing against the 2.1 m doors in views 8 and 10).
- **Plinth** about **0.6 m**, with 4–5 steps up to the portico and the end-block doors.
- **Eaves:** about **15.4 m** for the end blocks and centre block (4 floors); about **11.7 m** for the linking wings (3 floors). The centre block's roof is the biggest and highest, because its plan is the largest. Its eaves are level with the end blocks.

## Per face
**South (front, over the flag forecourt):** west end block · west wing · centre block · east wing · east end block.
- **West end block, S face:**
  - plain white wall;
  - a **full-height vertical glass strip** (about 2.4 m wide, dark teal glass in a white frame) near its east edge;
  - ground floor clad in **sandstone** with a glass entrance and steps.
- **West end block, W face:** 3 rows of small square windows (about 1.2 m), 3 per row, punched in white wall (views 3 and 16).
- **Linking wings, S face** (both alike, 6–7 bays each):
  - **open corridor galleries** on floors 1 and 2, behind white slab edges;
  - a **teal sloped canopy** (metal or glass, about 1.2 m deep) over each corridor floor;
  - thin steel railings;
  - **sandstone-clad piers** between the bays from ground to roof;
  - windows and doors set back behind the corridor.
- **Centre block, S face** (views 8, 11):
  - **ground:** a recessed **portico**: 4 square white columns about 0.8 m wide in front, a glass wall and sandstone piers behind;
  - **floors 1–3:** **continuous glass bands**, 3 wide bays per floor, each bay a 4-light window, between projecting **white slab bands** about 0.6 m deep and white piers;
  - **top:** glass up to the eaves.
  - The side faces of the centre block repeat the glass bands (3 bays).
  - There is **no curtain wall**: Gemini and LOOKS_LIKE's "glass curtain-wall bay" is wrong; it is banded glass between white slabs.
- **East end block, S face** (view 7):
  - plain white wall;
  - **full-height vertical glass strip** (about 2.4 m) near its west edge;
  - **round IIM emblem**, about 4.5 m across: a green sunburst ring with a blue and yellow centre, mounted on the wall;
  - green letters **"IIM / SHILLONG"** below the emblem;
  - ground floor sandstone with a glass entrance.
- **East end block, E face:** 3–4 small square windows per floor, white (DJI view).
- **North (back) faces:** not seen in any photo. The rear hall's walls are unknown. → photo list.

## Roof
- **Form:** **hip roofs** on every part (two end blocks, two wings, centre block). The **centre block's hip is the largest**; the wings carry long lower hips.
- **Pitch** low, about **18–22°**.
- **Eaves** deep, about **1.2–1.5 m**, with a white soffit and a **white fascia about 0.5 m** deep (view 7).
- **Colour:** green-teal corrugated metal sheet. It reads green from the air (DJI #789A8E in haze, graded video #0D9E9C); in overcast close-ups it looks grey-green.
- **Rear hall roof:** large and **dark grey** on the satellite (view 18); its form is a low hip or pitched roof, not confirmed.
- **Rooftop structures:** none visible on the hips.

## Surroundings
- **Flag forecourt:**
  - a horseshoe of **red-pink brick paving** around an oval lawn with a box hedge border and conifer topiary;
  - the lawn is cut by a cross of paths, with a **circular ring path** around the flag mast's round plinth in the centre;
  - the mast is about 30 m tall, with a large flag.
- **Lighting:** solar street lights around the forecourt edge.
- **Parking:** bays in front of the east block (SE), with cars, and near the west block.
- **Terraced garden** south of the forecourt: stepped grass terraces, stone retaining walls and winding paths (views 2, 5, 15).
- **Planters:** stone-edged raised beds along the wing fronts and the west block (views 7, 10).
- **Main road and Scholar's Path** to the west, below a stone retaining wall with a railing (view 16).

## LOOKS_LIKE re-check (the photos win)
- "Floors: 4": true for the end and centre blocks only; the linking wings have 3.
- "Glass curtain-wall bay over the main door": **wrong**. It is a portico with glass bands between white slabs.
- "Thin grey-green floor bands": the bands are **white** slab edges; the teal is the corridor canopies.
- "Light-grey stone plinth": **wrong**. The stone is **sandstone (tan/brown)**, on the wing piers, the end-block ground floors and the portico piers.
- "Vertical teal-glass strip" on both end blocks, the emblem on the east block, the green hips and white fascias: **confirmed**.
