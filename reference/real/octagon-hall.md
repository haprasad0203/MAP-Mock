# Octagonal Hall: real-building sheet (16 Nov 2026)
Montage [octagon-hall.jpg](octagon-hall.jpg). The Google satellite predates the hall, so the drone stills are the only source.
- **Footprint:** the real hall is a regular octagon about **27 m across**, measured against the 8 m road in 9QBtbam1G5Q @0:49.5. The data part is an **18 × 17 m rectangle**. The model draws an octagon inscribed in the data part.
  - Proposal for LAYOUT_CHECK: an octagon about 27 m across.
- **Position (proposal):** in the stills the hall stands on the loop road, opposite the flat-roofed service block (`road-block`), with two octagonal ponds behind it. In the data it is about 100 m west of `road-block` and 45 m south of the road.
- **Form:** one tall storey (about 5 m) of white wall with small high windows. At the door there is a **sandstone plinth band**, with steps and planters (0:07.5).
- **Roof:** a broad **octagonal pyramid** in green-teal ribbed sheet, with:
  - ridge caps on the eight hips;
  - a deep eave (about 1.5 m) with a white fascia;
  - a **small octagonal lantern cap** at the top.
- **Colours:** walls #EEEAE2, roof #3E7F75, stone #B8936A.
- **Surroundings:** a red hexagonal paver plaza out to the road kerb; two octagonal ponds behind.
- **Gaps:** the walls straight on, the door side, the inside of the plaza.
