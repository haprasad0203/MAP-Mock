# Hostel 2, F Block (new hostels): real-building sheet (16 Nov 2026)
Montage [hostel-2.jpg](hostel-2.jpg), fit [hostel-2_fit.png](hostel-2_fit.png). The satellite predates the blocks; the data parts follow the outline seen while they were under construction.
The same design is used for **Hostel 3** and **Hostel 4** (BUILDING_NOTES, H A), which have no photos of their own.
- **Floors:** 5 in the data. The photos show 4–5 above a stone base, where the ground falls.
- **Walls:** **peach-cream** plaster.
- **Long faces:** **balconies** with rounded white slab edges and dark rails, set in bays between **sandstone / brick-red stone piers**.
- **Stair towers:** full-height **blue-teal glass** shafts framed in white. They rise above the eaves and are topped with a small **green-capped pavilion**.
- **Entrance:** a stone-clad bay with a white **gable porch**, steps, and a ramp with steel rails (DSC_9144).
- **Courtyard side:** large glazed bays between the stone piers, and green-roofed porches (hostel22).
- **Roof:** **low green hip roofs** with wide white eaves.
- **Colours:** walls #EBCDB3, stone #A9765A, glass #2B5C6B, roof #3F8F80, eaves #F4F2EC.
- **Gaps:** the north faces, how the blocks join, and Hostels 3 and 4 themselves.
