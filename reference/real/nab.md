## Footprint and fit
- **Data parts:**
  - part 0 = NE end block (40.8 × 30.3 m, 5 floors, stoneTowers);
  - part 1 = middle range (59.6 × 18 m, 4 floors, atrium);
  - part 2 = SW end block (28.4 × 21.5 m, 5 floors, stoneTowers);
  - part 3 = a small SW piece (4 floors).
- **Fit** (`nab_fit.png`, pin fit residual about 5 m): the parts sit on the real roof outline; the long axis runs NE–SW.
- **Entrance mismatch, proposal for LAYOUT_CHECK:** the data's main entrance is at the **SW tip**, but the photos show the main doors on the **SE face**:
  - the central glass atrium;
  - two canopied side entrances, about 15 m either side of the atrium.

  All of these face the NAB lawn and the NAB front road. **Proposal:** a main entrance at the SE face centre (atrium), plus two side entrances. The SW tip may still have a road-side door (not seen).

## Floors and heights (views 1–2)
- **Main block:** **4 storeys** (ground plus 3), with a flat roof and parapet. Matches part 1's 4.
- **End towers** (one at each end of the SE face, about 6 m wide): stone shafts about **1 storey higher** than the main block, then a **white box storey** with a **green flared roof band**, then a **green hip cap**. In all about 5 storeys plus the cap, which matches the data's 5 for the end parts at the towers. The rest of the end blocks reads as 4 storeys.
- **Centre atrium pavilion:** a white box with a clerestory window band, under the **largest green pyramid hip**, rising about 1.5 storeys above the roof.
- **Two more small rooftop pavilions** (white with green hips) sit behind the stone panels, and two **white parapet boxes** with vertical slots sit over the white centre blocks (stair and lift heads).
- **Floor height** about **3.8 m**; **plinth** about 0.6 m, with steps at both canopies; **parapet** about 1.2 m.

   - topped by the white atrium pavilion and the green pyramid roof.

## Roof
- **Main roof:** flat, with a parapet.
- **Pavilions:** green hip caps; the atrium's is a **pyramid**, the largest.
- **Towers:** a green flared band at the top of the stone shaft, then the white box, then a green hip cap.
- **Colours:** the green is the same metal as Admin (green-teal; use **#3F8F80**); fascias white.

## Surroundings
- **Front plaza:** a red-pink paved strip about 8–10 m deep along the whole SE face, with steps at the two canopies, palms and solar lights.
- **NAB lawn:** a large flat lawn in front, with benches and a diagonal footpath (view 1).
- **NAB front road:** along the SE side (H A's marked view, white line).
- **Scholar's Path:** the covered walkway runs past the NAB's SW end (view 2, left).
- **NE end:** earthworks and new construction.
