## Updates from YouTube drone footage (see SOURCES.md for timestamps)
- **Octagonal hall**: larger than first assumed (~27 m), directly on the main road, west side, opposite a flat green-roofed service block; two small octagonal ponds behind. Name (Cafe Cloud Campus?) TC.
- **Admin Block**: confirmed facing south over the flag forecourt, with a big terraced garden with winding paths in front.
- **Court strip** (north-west, beside the hostels): teal-roof pavilion → blue tennis court → 2 basketball courts (green, red surround) → sand pit. The gym is probably the pavilion (TC).
- **Covered walkway**: a long teal-roofed walkway zig-zags from the hostels round the courts and down along the road — a key landmark for directions.
- **Football ground**: fenced bare earth with floodlights (as of the videos), north-east of the courts.
- **Type 2 Quarters**: ~9 small white 3-storey blocks with teal roofs, terraced on a hairpin road (staff, landmark only).
- **Hostel order**: the newest hostels (F Block / U Tirot Sing) sit highest on the northern ridge behind NAB; the order Hostel 1 (low) → 3 (high) still holds. TC.
- **Lake**: between the sports area and the academic block; the main road runs along its east side.
- Videos fix the arrangement, not distances: every position is still placed by eye, so Survey mode is still required.
