## Arrangement from Google Maps labels (by eye; no coordinates copied)
- **Students' Mess** and **Mithaas** (Commercial Complex) pins sit right beside **D Block**, west of the football ground. They have been moved there.
- **Faculty Quarters Type 3** (temporarily closed) is north-west of the hostels. Added as a staff landmark.
- **The Auditorium pin** sits on the large dark-roofed building directly north of the Admin forecourt circle, where the drone video shows the green-roofed Admin Block. Either the satellite image predates the roof, or the Auditorium is the big hall joined to the back of the Admin Block. **To confirm on campus.**
- The Google place photos for the Auditorium (an indoor event), the Mess (food) and D Block (none) have no exteriors.
