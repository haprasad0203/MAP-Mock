## Campus facts
- **Size and setting:** 120-acre campus at Umsawli, New Shillong, called the "campus in the clouds". Pine-covered hills and a lake with walking trails.
- **Opened:** inaugurated 18 Dec 2022.
- **Residences:** 4 hostel blocks. The U Tirot Sing Student Residence (2023) has four storeys, three blocks and a central common facility, with 216 single rooms.
- **Knowledge Centre (library):** over 10,000 sq ft; the reading room overlooks the pine hills.
- **Classrooms:** 22 rooms (4 × 40 seats, 12 × 60, 4 × 60 hybrid, 2 × 120 hybrid). Their locations aren't published.
- **Sports:** cricket-cum-football ground, plus basketball, volleyball, lawn tennis, badminton and throwball courts, hockey, and an open gym. Indoors: table tennis, pool, foosball, carrom, chess. A cycling track is planned.
- **Institute Guest House:** off campus at Lumjingsuk Homes, Mawdiangdiang, about 5 minutes' drive away.
- **Look, from photos and satellite:**
  - Cream and white plastered blocks with green pitched roofs.
  - Brown stone-clad pillars and glass stairwell towers on the newer hostels.
  - A large dark-grey auditorium roof.
  - Blue and green courts.
  - Grey roads winding over hills, dense pines all round.
